# 🦊 Eevee TCG Collector Checklist Pro

An interactive, responsive single-page web application designed for Pokémon Trading Card Game (TCG) collectors to track, organize, and manage their Eevee master collection.

Created by **Alonso Castillo Olguín**.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Dynamic Dual View:** Seamlessly toggle between a detailed **Table View** and an immersive **Gallery / Binder View**.
- **Hover Card Art Preview:** Hover over any card name in the table to display a high-resolution, floating thumbnail preview.
- **English Market Integration:** Instant 1-click links to search exact product listings and live market prices directly on **TCGPlayer**.
- **Collection Progress Tracker:** Real-time animated progress bar calculating unique completion percentage, owned copies, missing cards, and cards in transit.
- **Copy & Quantity Management:** Track duplicate cards with inline `+` / `−` counters to manage trade binders.
- **Wishlist with Priority:** Star cards you are actively hunting and assign priorities (*High*, *Normal*, *Low*) for local trades and events.
- **Era & Set Filters:** Quick-filter through Pokémon TCG historical eras from *Wizards of the Coast (WotC)* through *Scarlet & Violet*.
- **Custom Image URL Editor:** Add or update custom card art links directly from the interface.
- **Secure Local Storage:** All progress and edits are automatically saved to your browser's local storage without requiring account registration.
- **Backup & Portability:** Export your collection data to JSON or CSV for Excel, or restore your progress across different devices.
